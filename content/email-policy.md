title: Email policy
layout: policy
---

Mitchell Design Consultancy does not send unsolicited email. We email only people who have contacted us or who we work with, such as clients, consultants and contractors.

We do not keep a mailing list or send marketing email, and we do not buy, rent or share email addresses.

No one may advertise this website or Mitchell Design Consultancy in unsolicited email. This applies to us, to anyone who works for us, and to any partner, affiliate or other third party. No one is authorised to send promotional email on our behalf.

If you receive unsolicited email that mentions mitchelldesignconsultancy.com, please forward it with full headers to [simon@mitchelldesignconsultancy.com](mailto:simon@mitchelldesignconsultancy.com).
